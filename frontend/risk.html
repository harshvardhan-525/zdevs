How ratings are decided

Heat stressModerate: a day at 35°C or above. High: 40°C or above, or three days at 38°C or above.
Heavy rain / waterloggingModerate: 25 mm in a day or 40 mm over three days. High: 50 mm in a day or 80 mm over three days.
Dry spell / drought stressOne point each for: under 10 mm in the last 14 days, under 10 mm forecast for 7 days, 10+ days without rain, low modelled soil moisture. 2 points is Moderate, 3 or more is High. This is a short-term dry-spell signal, not a seasonal drought forecast.
Strong windModerate: 40 km/h or more. High: 60 km/h or more.
Cold / frostModerate: a night low of 2°C or below. High: 0°C or below.
Humid, damp weatherWatch: average humidity of 80% or more over 48 hours with 5 mm or more of rain in the next 3 days. Never rated higher, because it is only a weather signal, not a disease detection.
